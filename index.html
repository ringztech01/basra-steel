Manufacturing Capabilities

From Raw Plate
To Erected Steel

Our facility is laid out as a continuous line: material handling feeds CNC and plasma cutting, which feeds fit up and welding bays, blast and paint booths, and finally load out. Nothing waits, and nothing ships unverified.

  • CNC Beam Line Cutting
  • CNC Plasma & Laser
  • Robotic & Manual Welding
  • Plate Rolling & Bending
  • CNC Press Brakes
  • Shot / Sand Blasting
  • Enclosed Paint Booths
