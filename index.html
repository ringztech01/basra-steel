Solar & Energy

Sun-Powered Steel Production

Basra Steel engaged ICC, a regional solar and inverter systems company, to build one of the largest captive solar installations in Iraq's industrial sector.

ICC Solar Installation

In 2026, ICC completed the design, installation and commissioning of a 47MW solar power system at Basra Steel's industrial facility in Basra, Iraq. The system powers critical production lines and site infrastructure, reducing reliance on grid electricity and diesel generation.

The project was executed turnkey by ICC, covering civil works, structural mounting, high voltage interconnection and a battery energy storage system for load stabilisation.
